Every seed starts from its own corrected full-communication baseline actor. Training stops when both targets pass validation and an independent confirmation set, or the step ceiling is exhausted. Final evaluation uses 200 baseline-matched held-out episodes; a confirmation pass does not guarantee a held-out pass. Collisions do not terminate episodes. Collision step rate is the fraction of the 100 steps with any overlapping agent pair.
